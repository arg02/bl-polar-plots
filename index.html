How to read the wind

Polar plots

A year of pollution, drawn by the wind

Basemap styles need Mapbox (OSM fallback in use).

50%

These plots show how pollution at this sensor relates to wind over a full year.

  • Direction around the circle is where the wind came from (N, E, S, W).
  • Distance from the centre is wind speed — calm near the middle, stronger winds toward the edge.
  • Colour shows the average concentration (µg/m³) for that wind combination — not the yearly site average. Warmer colours can sit well above the annual mean because they reflect specific wind conditions when levels were higher.

Look for warmer patches to see which directions pollution tends to arrive from. Switch between NO2 and PM2.5 above the plot — patterns can differ by pollutant.
